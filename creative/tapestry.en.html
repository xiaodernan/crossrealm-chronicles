Timeline

The Crossrealm Chronicles — seven chapters + prologue + four letters (*A Letter from the Fire* · *The Keeper’s Reply* · *Lin’s Reply to the Keeper* · *The Reply of the One Who Came After*), in the order the story happens. Brackets point to the game chapters and OST landing points.

  1. Prologue
    Prologue · One Hundred and Nine Echoes
    The first pulse of the World Anchor. The turbulence across the realms was never an accident — it was a single sentence nobody had finished reading. The inbox-shaped slot on the anchor stays empty, and the emptiness is the invitation.
    OST 01 «Anchor Pulse»
  2. Chapter One
    Chapter One · The Intersection Hub
    Three ancient paths sink into three skies. A campfire of the elfin scout in the Greywood, the unopened letter on the Miller Wastes, a floating candle in the Magic Realm — the traveller presses F for the first time, and hears the wind remember his name for the first time.
    OST 02 «Three Roads» · 06 «Campfire Ember»
  3. Chapter Two
    Chapter Two · The Broken Timeline and Chaos Fringe
    Nineteen eighty-three, playing on a loop on the radio — nobody remembers what happened that year, and everyone is waiting for it to happen again. Upside spores make the shadow move first; the clocktower tolls at 11:06.
    OST 11 «Miller’s Hourglass» · 13 «1983 Loop» · 15 «11:06 Bell»
  4. Chapter Three
    Chapter Three · The People in the Campfire’s Memory
    The hearth needs no fuel — because it feeds on the shape of the past. By the fire, the traveller first hears the two voices of ‘keep the light’ and ‘put it out’ — and in both voices, a version of themselves.
    OST 06 · OST 23 «Full Resonance»
  5. Chapter Four
    Chapter Four · The Keeper
    The Keeper never speaks. Not because he can’t — he hands one splinter of his own silence to every traveller who drifts through, because he knows the only thing that lets someone put their sword down is hearing, undisturbed, their own voice.
    OST 04 «Stone Ring Chant» · 16 «Spiral Count»
  6. Chapter Five
    Chapter Five · The One Who Writes
    After the ending, Lin Du begins to write. The first letter — to the deepest dark of the moon; the second — to travellers not yet born; the third — to himself. In the middle of the writing, he starts to cry.
    OST 10 «Murphy’s Stay» · 24 «Infinity Walker»
  7. Chapter Six
    Chapter Six · Echoes
    What is an echo? A mountain telling you what shape your line has. The wilderness is a wider mountain — it tells the traveller what self they are willing to see.
    OST 23 «Full Resonance» · OST 24 «Infinity Walker»
  8. Finale
    Chapter Seven · Embers
    The waystation notice board: we take no letters here, only wayfarers. At the rim of the star-map a filament-thin silver crack hangs between Old God Whisper and Starport Trade — the fire gives the next lamp one direction.
    OST 25 «Ember Low Hum»
  9. Interlude I
    A Letter from the Fire to You
    Five short letters written by the fire, addressed to everyone who has pressed E: why you came here, why the fire never dies — that you are already the candidate — and a word about what comes next.
    OST 25 «Ember Low Hum»
  10. Interlude II
    The Keeper’s Reply
    A letter written from the other side of the mist — addressed to those who haven’t pressed E yet: the mist is a thousand-year-old held breath; the Keeper never speaks first; the lamp he relights between saves; and the question that was asked wrong — ‘which keeps more?’
    OST 25 «Ember Low Hum»
  11. Interlude III
    Lin Du’s Reply to the Keeper
    The third and final letter of the trilogy — after the fire wrote, after the fog wrote — the one who walked out of the fire begins to write: carrying fire to the end of the wastes, painting the Lamp Road onto the world, ‘the door is you’; the Keeper was never guarding the flame — he was waiting for the next Lin Du.
    OST 25 «Ember Low Hum»
  12. Interlude IV
    The Reply of the One Who Came After
    The one who read all three letters picks up the pen — nameless, with no verdict, no conclusion; pressing a fourth page by the silver filament at the rim of the star-map: you are not the keeper of the hearth; you are the next lamp.
    OST 25 «Ember Low Hum»
  13. Interlude V
    Before the Flame
    The canon’s rough draft: nine unlit lamps adrift in the Before — a traveler carried the last unconfiscated coal of the Dominion across the wastes; a hundred and eight who walked before him set it down, one didn’t; that is how the hundred and nine echoes came to be. When the fire fell, it said One Realm to bind them all — the mist was born from words never spoken; the Keeper was born because someone finally sat down.
    OST 01 «Anchor Flame»
