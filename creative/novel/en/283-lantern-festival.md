## Two Hundred and Eighty-Three: Lantern Festival

The fifteenth day of the twelfth month — Tuesday — night — the fire burned.

The whole village gathered in the square — lanterns launched — he said: " — lantern festival —"; his mother said: " — lantern festival —" — the fire burned — they formed a circle — danced round — he said: " — lantern dance —"; his mother said: " — lantern dance —" — the fire burned — children on hobbyhorses — he said: " — hobbyhorse fly —"; his mother said: " — hobbyhorse fly —" — the fire burned — deep into the night — bell chimes — he said: " — longer night —"; his mother said: " — longer night —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Lantern festival.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
