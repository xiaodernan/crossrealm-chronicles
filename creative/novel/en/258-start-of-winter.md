## Two Hundred and Fifty-Eight: Start of Winter

The forty-second week after the spring equinox — Thursday — early morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the wind was hard — the ground had cooled — he said: “ — start of winter —” — his mother said: “ — start of winter —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the well — there was frost on the rim of the well — he said: “ — the well is rimmed with white —” — his mother said: “ — the well is rimmed with white —” — the fire burned — A-Shi came back — he carried an armful of firewood — he said: “ — winter has come —” — his mother said: “ — winter has come —” — the fire burned — his mother fed one more stick into the stove — the fire smiled — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — the firewood is plenty —” — his mother said: “ — the firewood is plenty —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Start of winter.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
