## Two Hundred and Twenty-Nine: The Dough

The thirteenth week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — his mother had already brought the flour to the kitchen table — she poured the flour into the bowl — she added water — she added a little salt — she began to knead — he stood beside her — he watched — his mother kneaded — the flour became dough — the dough was soft — soft enough — he said: “ — the dough —” — his mother said: “ — the dough —” — she covered the dough — she said: “ — wait a while —” — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — wait a while —” — his mother said: “ — wait a while —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The dough.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
