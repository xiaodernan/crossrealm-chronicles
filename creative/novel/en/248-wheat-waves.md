## Two Hundred and Forty-Eight: Wheat Waves

The thirty-second week after the spring equinox — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the field was wheat — a stretch of it had turned gold — the wind passed — the wheat bent — he said: “ — wheat waves —” — his mother said: “ — wheat waves —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the ridge of the field — he stood still — he watched — the wind passed — the wheat lifted in one sweep — one layer — then another — he said: “ — layer upon layer —” — his mother called from the doorway: “ — layer upon layer —” — the fire burned — A-Shi bent down — he ran his hand over an ear of wheat — the ear was full — he said: “ — it is full —” — his mother said: “ — it is full —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Wheat waves.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
