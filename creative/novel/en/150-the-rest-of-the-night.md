## One Hundred and Fifty: The Rest of the Night

Tuesday of the twenty-fifth week after the spring equinox — deep night — only the firelight left.

A-Shi kept the watch through the night — he did not sleep — toward dawn — he dozed off against the stove's edge.

He did not know when he had fallen asleep — he only remembered the fire was still there — the fire burned.

He woke — it was still dark — the fire was still there — the fire burned.

His mother was not in the room — he did not know when she had gone out — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood up — he looked at the fire — it had not gone out — he sat again — he looked at the fire again.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

He counted — one — two — three — he counted to seven — he stopped — he counted again — one — two — three — he counted to seven — he stopped.

The fire burned.

He thought — keeping the watch is not about not sleeping — it is about not leaving.

He looked at the fire again — the fire burned — he straightened the old lamp a little — the wick burnt — there was no oil — the fire burned.

His mother came back — she stood at the doorway — she looked at him — she said nothing — the fire burned.

His mother said: “The fire did not go out.”

A-Shi said: “The fire did not go out.”

His mother came over — she sat down — she watched the fire — she said: “Keeping the watch is not about the fire — it is about the fire still being here.”

The fire burned.

His mother said again: “You fell asleep — and it did not go out.”

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The sky brightened little by little — the fire burned.
