## One Hundred and Twenty-two: Always Here

Week seven after the spring equinox — Saturday — overcast, heavy with that low moisture that doesn't quite rain and never does. The stove burned steadily.

Mother was frying cabbage and pork dumplings in the pan — the steady sizzle of fat against hot iron, the kind of sound the house had known for generations.

She set the dumpling filling aside and started doing other preparations with more than her usual quietness. A-Shi crouched by the stove, watching the fire. He didn't speak either.

Xiao Huo scratched his eyes, then saw the doorway.

There stood Snow'an's mother at the gate.

Behind her were three children — two girls in blue jackets, one boy in grey. They stood there without speaking. All the light in the doorway came from the stove behind them.

Mother moved from kitchen to door and saw them.

Snow'an's mother smiled. She set a small cloth basket on the stove — the contents covered by a cotton cloth. She said nothing. She only looked once at A-Shi.

A-Shi didn't speak either.

The three children's faces glowed softly from within as they watched the stove.

"Come in and sit," Mother said.

The children stepped in. They watched the fire.

The cabbage stewed in the pan smelled rich and salty in the warm room.

Mother brought three stools and lined them up beside the stove. The children sat side by side. They watched the fire — they watched — they watched — they watched.

Outside, the fire-seed tree was making that particular sound leaves do when they are wet and wind-carries.

Mother approached the stove a third time. This time she crouched. She didn't speak.

Snow'an's mother unwrapped the cloth bundle. Inside were three pairs of cotton shoes — the needlework identical — the stitches identical — brand new.

Mother didn't speak. She picked up the smallest pair, then the largest one — her hand journeyed up the sizes.

Snow'an's mother said only:

"These are from the house my mother came from."

No one asked which house.

Years ago — that old pot — when it was made — the fire in the stove had been going in her hands too.

Everyone in the kitchen didn't ask.

Outside, that small stream came sliding through the ditch.

The smell of coal smoke drifted from the stove.

炉火.
