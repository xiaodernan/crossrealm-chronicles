## Two Hundred and Twenty-Eight: The Mill

The twelfth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — he carried the ears to the mill — the old mill — stone walls — he poured the ears into the stone mortar — he began to mill — it was slow — it was very slow — it was very slow — his mother stood beside him — the fire burned — he stopped — he looked at his hands — his hands were covered in flour — he said: “ — the mill —” — his mother said: “ — the mill —” — he milled again — it was slow — it was very slow — the fire burned — there was flour on the floor — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The mill.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
