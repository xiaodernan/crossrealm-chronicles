## Two Hundred and Thirty-Six: The Ember

The twentieth week after the spring equinox — Friday — night — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — inside the belly of the hearth — there was nothing but a ring of ash — the ash was red at the edge — in the middle — one coal — glowing — he knelt down — he looked — his mother stood behind him — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — an ember —” — his mother said: “ — an ember —” — he looked — the red one — he said: “ — still glowing —” — his mother said: “ — still glowing —” — the fire burned — A-Shi put his head into the mouth of the hearth — he watched — it glowed — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The ember.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
