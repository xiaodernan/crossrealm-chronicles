## Two Hundred and Twenty-Three: The Pollination

The seventh week after the spring equinox — Sunday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fields were green — the wind was light — the wind came — the ears of wheat swayed in the wind — they swung wide — he watched — he said: “ — the pollination —” — his mother said: “ — the pollination —” — the wind came — the wind went — the fire burned — the ears were in the wind — they swayed three times — he said: “ — it is fragrant —” — his mother said: “ — it is fragrant —” — the fire burned — the wind stopped — he said: “ — it is done —” — his mother said: “ — it is done —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The pollination.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
