## One Hundred and Eighty-six: The Door Opened

The third week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi opened the door — he did not call out — the hinge sounded of itself — the fire burned.

Outside — the smell of breakfast, birch leaves, and a plume of white smoke — his mother stood on the porch — her hands were empty — she said: “ — Azu said —”

A-Shi said: “ — what —”

His mother said: “ — those who can stay — find every door opening on its own —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I can stay —”

His mother said: “ — Azu said — only if you can stay does the door open —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the birch stood a little taller — the fire burned — he did not go in — the fire burned — he stayed by the door — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
