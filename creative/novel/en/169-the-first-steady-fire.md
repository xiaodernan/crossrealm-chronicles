## One Hundred and Sixty-nine: The First Steady Fire

The ninth day — before first light — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — the first thing he did was look at the stove — the fire burned.

His mother was not there — she was in the yard — A-Shi walked over — she sat on the stone block — holding a piece of charcoal — she lifted it up — showed it to him.

A-Shi said: “What is this?”

His mother said: “Your Azu — the day he died — he gave it to me.”

A-Shi said: “Did he say anything?”

His mother said: “He did. He said — you tend the fire not to watch it — you keep it because the fire remembers —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

His mother placed the charcoal in his hand — she said: “This one — keep it. Burn it until it is only ash — then add more.”

A-Shi said: “Why this one?”

His mother said: “Because Azu lit it —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
