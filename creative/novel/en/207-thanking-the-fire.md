## Two Hundred and Seven: Thanking the Fire

The tenth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi added wood — the flame rose — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — thank it —” — A-Shi said: “ — thank it —” — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — thank you —”

The fire burned.

His mother said: “ — the fire is listening — it knows —”

The fire burned.

Thanking the fire.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
