## Two Hundred and Eighty-Eight: Flowering

The twelfth day after start of spring — Sunday — daytime — the fire burned.

Flowers everywhere in the village — all blossoms competing — he said: " — flowering —"; his mother said: " — flowering —" — the fire burned — A-Shi at the field edge — picking wild flowers — he said: " — flowers bloom —"; his mother said: " — flowers bloom —" — the fire burned — A-Shi weaving a flower garland — he said: " — garland —"; his mother said: " — garland —" — the fire burned — children chasing butterflies — he said: " — butterfly dance —"; his mother said: " — butterfly dance —" — the fire burned — sunset — lamps lit — he said: " — gentle night —"; his mother said: " — gentle night —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Flowering.
