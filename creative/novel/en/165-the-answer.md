## One Hundred and Sixty-five: The Answer

Late at night — the fire burned.

His mother slept — no sound from the room.

A-Shi rose from his bed — walked to the stove — he stared at the quiet ring of fire — he said softly: “I know.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi crouched down — from the corner bundle of wood — one log — two logs — he laid them gently onto the fire.

The fire did not burst — it only brightened a little — as if it had heard something.

A-Shi said: “I will keep adding wood.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not go back to bed — he sat before the stove — he was not sleepy anymore — he watched the fire — he counted the five characters — “The seven-hundred-and-first year, up to here” — he counted them one hundred times.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
