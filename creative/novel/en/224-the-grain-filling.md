## Two Hundred and Twenty-Four: The Grain Filling

The eighth week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fields were green — the ears were bending down — a little more than yesterday — he reached out — he touched a grain — the grain had hardened — he said: “ — the grain is filling —” — his mother said: “ — the grain is filling —” — the wind was light — the wind stopped — the fire burned — A-Shi squatted down — he looked at the ears — he looked at his own hands — he said: “ — they are hard —” — his mother said: “ — they are hard —” — the fire burned — the ears were in the wind — the wind came — the wind went — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The grain is filling.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
