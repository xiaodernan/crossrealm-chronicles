## Two Hundred and Thirty-Seven: The Wind Bell

The twenty-first week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi walked out of the lane — from the west a man came — the man wore a traveler's cloak — the cloak was covered in dust — he pushed open the door — he said: “ — inn —” — the innkeeper said: “ — inn —” — the man sat down — he ordered a bowl of porridge — the innkeeper said: “ — the porridge is ready —” — the traveler took one bite — the traveler said: “ — what rides the wind today —” — the innkeeper said: “ — what —” — the traveler said: “ — the west wind — it carries the smell of birch —” — A-Shi stood at the doorway — A-Shi said: “ — birch —” — the traveler said: “ — birch —” — A-Shi walked out — he said: “ — the anchor fire —” — the traveler looked at him — the traveler said: “ — I have seen it — it is not far now —” — A-Shi said: “ — it is not far now —” — the innkeeper said: “ — it is not far now —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The wind bell.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
