## Two Hundred and Seventy-Six: Cold Dew

The eleventh day after start of autumn — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — dew drops on grass froze — he said: “ — cold dew —” — his mother said: “ — cold dew —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — grass tips white as frost — icy to the touch — he said: “ — cold dew has fallen —”; his mother said: “ — cold dew has fallen —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — water colder — he said: “ — colder water —”; his mother said: “ — colder water —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — frost will come —”; A-Shi said: “ — frost will come —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —”; A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Cold dew.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
