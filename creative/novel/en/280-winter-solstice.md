## Two Hundred and Eighty: Winter Solstice

The twenty-fifth day after start of autumn — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — ice pillars on the ground — he said: " — winter solstice —"; his mother said: " — winter solstice —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — ice pillars hanging from the eaves — he said: " — ice drips —"; his mother said: " — ice drips —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — hot soup — he said: " — hot soup —"; his mother said: " — hot soup —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — the new year —"; A-Shi said: " — the new year —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Winter solstice.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
