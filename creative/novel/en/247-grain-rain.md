## Two Hundred and Forty-Seven: Grain Rain

The thirty-first week after the spring equinox — Saturday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there were clouds in the sky — the rain was near — he said: “ — it is going to rain —” — his mother said: “ — it is near —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he looked up — he waited — the fire burned — the fire burned — after a while — drops fell — drops fell on the iron at the mouth of the hearth — he said: “ — it has begun —” — his mother said: “ — it has begun —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the river — he said: “ — the river is a little higher —” — his mother said: “ — the river is a little higher —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain rain.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
