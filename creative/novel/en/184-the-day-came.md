## One Hundred and Eighty-four: The Day Came

The second week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — the fire burned — he did not look — he dressed — he sat before the stove — his mother was not in the room — the fire burned — he did not add wood — he watched — the fire did not move.

He did not move — the fire burned.

His mother returned — carrying something in her hands.

She said: “She has come.”

A-Shi said: “Who?”

His mother said: “The one who can stay — she has come.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — she?”

His mother said: “ — Azu —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
