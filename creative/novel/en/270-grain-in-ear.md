## Two Hundred and Seventy: Grain in Ear

The ninth day after summer begins — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the rain had fallen through the night — there was more water in the field — he said: “ — grain in ear —” — his mother said: “ — grain in ear —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the ridge of the field — the rice seedlings were set into the ground — row upon row — the water clung to his hands — he said: “ — plant faster —” — his mother said: “ — plant faster —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi finished planting — he shook out his hands — he said: “ — it is planted —” — his mother said: “ — it is planted —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the hour of sowing has come —” — A-Shi said: “ — the hour of sowing has come —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain in ear.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
