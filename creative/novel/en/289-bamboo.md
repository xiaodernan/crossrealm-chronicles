## Two Hundred and Eighty-Nine: Bamboo

The nineteenth day after start of summer — Saturday — daytime — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — bamboo forest — he said: " — bamboo —"; his mother said: " — bamboo —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the forest — bamboo shoots growing — he said: " — bamboo grows —"; his mother said: " — bamboo grows —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi snapped a section — he said: " — bamboo section —"; his mother said: " — bamboo section —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — section by section higher —"; A-Shi said: " — section by section higher —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Bamboo.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
