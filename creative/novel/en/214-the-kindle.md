## Two Hundred and Fourteen: The Kindle

The first week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the fire burned — he watched the flame — the fire burned — he said: “ — the kindle —”

His mother said: “ — the kindle —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — keeping the fire —”

His mother said: “ — keeping the fire —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I remember —”

His mother said: “ — you remember —”

The fire burned.

The kindle.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
