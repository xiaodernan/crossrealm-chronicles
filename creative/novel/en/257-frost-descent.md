## Two Hundred and Fifty-Seven: Frost Descent

The forty-first week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was white frost on the grass — he said: “ — frost descent —” — his mother said: “ — frost descent —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — he looked at the grass — it was white — he reached out — the frost did not stick — he said: “ — it is white —” — his mother said: “ — it is white —” — the fire burned — after a while — the sun came out — the frost melted — the grass showed green — he said: “ — the frost has melted —” — his mother said: “ — the frost has melted —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — winter draws near —” — A-Shi said: “ — winter draws near —” — his mother said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — A-Shi said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Frost descent.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
