## Two Hundred and Forty-Five: The Clouds Move

The twenty-ninth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — he looked up — the clouds had moved — he said: “ — the clouds —” — his mother said: “ — the clouds —” — the fire burned — A-Shi watched the clouds drift — he said: “ — go —” — his mother said: “ — go —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked again — another one — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — one after another —” — his mother said: “ — one after another —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The clouds move.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
