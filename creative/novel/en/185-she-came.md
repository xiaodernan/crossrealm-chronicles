## One Hundred and Eighty-five: She Came

The second week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the fire burned — his mother was not in the room — A-Shi did not speak — there was only the fire and the wind — the fire burned.

The kitchen door opened — his mother, in her hand — she held nothing — she only reached out — she rested her hand on A-Shi’s head — she said: “She has come —”

A-Shi said: “ — she —”

His mother said: “ — Azu —”

A-Shi said: “ — Azu —”

His mother said: “ — yes —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
