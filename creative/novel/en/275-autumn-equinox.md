## Two Hundred and Seventy-Five: Autumn Equinox

The seventh day after start of autumn — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — shadows were equal — he said: “ — autumn equinox —” — his mother said: “ — autumn equinox —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — day and night of equal length — he said: “ — day and night are equal —”; his mother said: “ — day and night are equal —” — the fire burned — after a while — the wind no longer like summer — not so hot — he said: “ — the wind has softened —”; his mother said: “ — the wind has softened —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the hour of harvest —”; A-Shi said: “ — the hour of harvest —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —”; A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Autumn equinox.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
