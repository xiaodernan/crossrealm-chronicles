## Two Hundred and Sixty-Six: Awakening of Insects

The second week after spring begins — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was a sound in the sky — as if something had been stirred awake — he said: “ — the thunder stirs —” — his mother said: “ — the thunder stirs —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the snow had melted — the ground had softened — the grass was covered with a thin layer of green — he said: “ — the grass is green —” — his mother said: “ — the grass is green —” — the fire burned — after a while — there was a smell of soil in the wind — and insects — A-Shi crouched down — he looked — he said: “ — the insects have stirred —” — his mother said: “ — the insects have stirred —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked again — beneath the roots of the grass — he said: “ — everything is alive —” — his mother said: “ — everything is alive —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Awakening of insects.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
