## One Hundred and Seventy: The First Night

The next day — late at night — the fire burned.

A-Shi had fallen asleep.

He was slumped in the chair — still holding the charcoal in his hand — his mother came out from the room — she crouched down — she did not take the charcoal from his hand — she only laid the old blanket over his legs — the fire burned.

She said softly: “Keep the fire —”

A-Shi did not open his eyes — something crossed his lips.

His mother looked at the fire for a long time — the fire burned.

She did not speak again — she only — sat back in her place — waiting for the morning — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
