## One Hundred and Seventy-five: The Wick Is Fine

The tenth day — before first light — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — he saw the old oil lamp — it stood on the table — unlit — he did not move — he said softly: “Ma —”

No — it was his mother’s voice — she said: “Look at the wick —”

A-Shi said: “It’s very fine.”

His mother said: “It is very fine — you do not need to look closely — it is waiting for you —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi walked over — he picked the lamp up — he set it back on the table — he said nothing — the fire burned.

His mother said: “You keep the fire — you keep this too — slowly —”

A-Shi said: “Slowly —”

His mother said: “Yes.”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
