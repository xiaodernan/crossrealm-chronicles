## One Hundred and Eighty-three: The Sky Brightened

The second week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the sky brightened.

The fire burned.

A-Shi woke — he opened the window — he looked outside — the birch was still there — it had not changed — but it stood a little taller.

His mother said: “It will speak.”

A-Shi said: “ — speak.”

His mother said: “ — the way — whatever can be kept — can be seen clearly —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — good.”

His mother said: “ — the sparks burned once — they are recorded — you remember them too —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I remember.”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
