## Two Hundred and Seventeen: The Lamp Went Out

The first week after the spring equinox — Saturday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the oil lamp was lit — the fire burned — no light at night — no wind at night — the fire burned — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — the fire —” — his mother said: “ — keep it —” — A-Shi said: “ — the fire —” — the fire burned — he did not add wood — the fire burned — the flame grew small — the lamp went out — the fire burned.

He remembered — the fire burned — the fire is — this thing — the fire burned.

The lamp went out — the fire burned.

The lamp went out.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
