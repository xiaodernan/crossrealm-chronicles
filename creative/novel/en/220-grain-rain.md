## Two Hundred and Twenty: Grain Rain

The fourth week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sky was just brightening — the sky was still grey — the fire burned — he listened — there was a faint sound — a very light sound — it was rain — falling on the birch leaves — finer than needles — he reached out — he caught a few drops — he lowered his hand — the fire burned — he said: “ — grain rain —” — his mother said: “ — grain rain —” — the fire burned — the rain fell — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain rain.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
