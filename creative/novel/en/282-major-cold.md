## Two Hundred and Eighty-Two: Major Cold

The thirtieth day after start of autumn — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — thick ice on the ground — he said: " — major cold —"; his mother said: " — major cold —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — thick ice has formed — he said: " — ice has formed —"; his mother said: " — ice has formed —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — scalding water — he said: " — scalding water —"; his mother said: " — scalding water —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — the year has turned —"; A-Shi said: " — the year has turned —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.
