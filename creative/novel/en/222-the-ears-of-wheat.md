## Two Hundred and Twenty-Two: The Ears of Wheat

The sixth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fields were green — they were taller already — she said — he listened — his mother said: “ — the ears of wheat —” — he said: “ — the ears of wheat —” — A-Shi walked to the field — he squatted down — he touched the shoots — the grains were still inside the sheath — the sheath was wrinkled — he said: “ — they are coming out of the sheath —” — his mother said: “ — they are coming out of the sheath —” — the fire burned — he rested his palm over the heads — he said: “ — soon —” — his mother said: “ — soon —” — the fire burned — A-Shi put the last grain into his pocket — he said: “ — wait —” — his mother said: “ — wait —” — the fire burned.

They came out of the sheath.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
