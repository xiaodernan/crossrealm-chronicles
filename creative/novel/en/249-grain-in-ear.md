## Two Hundred and Forty-Nine: Grain in Ear

The thirty-third week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the field was wheat — the waves of wheat rose again — the awns were out — he said: “ — grain in ear —” — his mother said: “ — grain in ear —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the ridge of the field — he bent down — he looked — the ears were thick with awns — he said: “ — the awns are out —” — his mother said: “ — the awns are out —” — the fire burned — after a while — the wind passed — the wheat lifted in rippling rows — he said: “ — the wind is here —” — his mother said: “ — the wind is here —” — the fire burned — A-Shi straightened up — he dusted his hands — a broken awn clung to them — very fine — he said: “ — a prick —” — his mother said: “ — a prick —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain in ear.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
