## Two Hundred and Sixteen: Spring

The first week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the birch had sprouted leaves — they were about the size of a thumb — the fire burned — his mother stood at the kitchen table — she did not speak — the fire burned — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — spring —”

His mother said: “ — spring —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — the fire must be kept —”

His mother said: “ — the fire must be kept —”

The fire burned.

Spring.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
