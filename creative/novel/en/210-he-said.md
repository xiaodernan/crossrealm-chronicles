## Two Hundred and Ten: He Said

The thirteenth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

The birch sounded — once — louder than usual — the fire burned — his mother stood at the door — the fire burned.

She did not speak — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — who —”

His mother said: “ — Azu —”

A-Shi said: “ — Azu —”

His mother said: “ — he said those who keep the fire — must not stop —”

A-Shi said: “ — not stop —”

His mother said: “ — not stop —”

The fire burned.

He said it.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
