## One Hundred and Sixty-seven: The Steady Hand

The next day — before first light — the fire burned.

His mother carried a log out to the yard — she set it on the stone block — she handed an axe to A-Shi.

A-Shi took it — he did not move.

His mother said: “First you watch —”

His mother raised the axe — she did not chop — she only stood there — she said: “Fire does not choose hands — steady hands do —”

A-Shi said: “What does steady mean?”

His mother said: “A log falling on the ground does not hurt — a hand can — so steady first — then let fall —”

A-Shi nodded — he said nothing — the fire burned.

His mother put the axe in A-Shi’s hand — she gripped his hand together with hers — she said: “Follow me —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
