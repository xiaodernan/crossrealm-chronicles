## Two Hundred and Eleven: He Heard It

The thirteenth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi kept the stove — the fire burned — his eyes were open — the fire burned — he did not sleep — the fire burned.

He heard it — the fire burned — that sound — lighter than an ordinary fire — farther than an ordinary fire — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I am listening —”

The fire burned.

He heard it.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
