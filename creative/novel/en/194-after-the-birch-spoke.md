## One Hundred and Ninety-four: After the Birch Spoke

The fourth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — the fire burned — the birch was quiet now — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother cooked breakfast — the fire burned — she did not speak — the fire burned — he heard the pot of water begin to sing — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — what did the birch say —”

His mother said: “ — the birch said — the fire must be like this —”

A-Shi said: “ — like this —”

His mother said: “ — like this —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi sat down before the stove — the fire burned — his mother sat beside him — the fire burned — she said: “ — the vows of the seven-hundred-and-second year — the birch keeps them for you —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not ask — the fire burned — when the birch had said it — the fire burned — he knew — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
