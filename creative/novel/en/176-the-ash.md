## One Hundred and Seventy-six: The Ash

The eleventh day — before first light — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — he did not add wood — he crouched before the stove — he did not move — he saw a piece of paper on the ground — it was very old — it was curled — it was in the corner — he picked it up — he held it in his palm.

His mother was in the room — she said: “That was Azu’s paper —”

A-Shi said: “Can it catch?”

His mother said: “It can catch — it would burn away —”

A-Shi said: “Then keep it for what?”

His mother said: “Keep it not — the one who keeps the fire — need not keep it —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
