## One Hundred and Seventy-three: Something Happened

The eighth day — late at night — the fire burned.

A-Shi did not sleep — he sat before the stove — his mother pushed the door open — holding a bowl of soup — she looked at him — she said: “Azu, the day he died, was keeping the fire too.”

A-Shi said: “Did he keep it?”

His mother said: “ — he kept it. The next year — the fire had not gone out —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

His mother put the soup in A-Shi’s hand — she said: “Fire doesn’t choose the person — it chooses the one who is willing to stay —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi lowered his head — he finished the soup — he put the bowl away — he said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
