## Two Hundred and Thirty-Four: The Kindle

The eighteenth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fire was low — in the belly of the hearth — the ash was thin too — he brought the branches over — he picked three — he placed the first branch — into the hearth — the fire burned — he said: “ — feed the fire —” — his mother said: “ — feed the fire —” — he placed the second branch in — the fire burned — the flame grew — he said: “ — it grew —” — his mother said: “ — it grew —” — the third branch — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Feed the fire.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
