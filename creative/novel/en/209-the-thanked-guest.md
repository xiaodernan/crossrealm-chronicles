## Two Hundred and Nine: The Thanked Guest

The twelfth week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

The door sounded — the servant Ah-Jin said: “ — a guest —” — A-Shi went to open it — outside the wind was hard — an old man stood — his hands were empty — the fire burned.

The old man said: “ — the fire is still alive —”

A-Shi said: “ — it is —”

The old man said: “ — it told you —”

A-Shi said: “ — it did —”

The fire burned.

The old man left — the fire burned — Ah-Jin said: “ — who was that — nobody knows him —” — A-Shi said: “ — the fire knows —” — the fire burned.

The thanked guest.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
