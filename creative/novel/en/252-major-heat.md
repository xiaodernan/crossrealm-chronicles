## Two Hundred and Fifty-Two: Major Heat

The thirty-sixth week after the spring equinox — Friday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun was fierce — the ground was white — he said: “ — major heat —” — his mother said: “ — major heat —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the shade of the tree — the cicadas sang — he said: “ — the cicadas are singing —” — his mother said: “ — the cicadas are singing —” — the fire burned — A-Shi brought out a basin of water — he set it at the doorway — sweat dripped into the water — he said: “ — it is at its hottest —” — his mother said: “ — it is at its hottest —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — major heat is passing —” — A-Shi said: “ — major heat is passing —” — his mother said: “ — the beginning of autumn draws near —” — A-Shi said: “ — the beginning of autumn draws near —” — the fire burned — after a while — a few clouds gathered — a gust of wind rose — a sliver of sun was hidden by the cloud — he said: “ — clouds are coming —” — his mother said: “ — clouds are coming —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Major heat.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
