## Two Hundred and Twenty-One: The Wheat Grain

The fifth week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fields were green — they were up past the ankles — taller than chopsticks — his mother and A-Shi walked to the fields early in the morning — she looked at them — she did not speak — A-Shi squatted down — he ran his hand through — he picked up a wheat grain — it was very light — he said: “ — grain —” — his mother said: “ — grain —” — A-Shi put it in his pocket — he said: “ — a seed —” — his mother said: “ — a seed —” — the fire burned — he took it home — the fire burned.

The wheat grain.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
