## Two Hundred and Seventy-Two: Minor Heat

The sixteenth day after summer begins — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — a wave of heat surged over — he said: “ — minor heat —” — his mother said: “ — minor heat —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the wheat had turned yellow — the ground scorched his feet — he said: “ — it is nearly sun-dried —” — his mother said: “ — it is nearly sun-dried —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi drew a bucket of well water — ice-cold — he splashed it at the foot of the courtyard wall — he said: “ — the wall is cool now —” — his mother said: “ — the wall is cool now —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — what a hot day —” — A-Shi said: “ — what a hot day —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Minor heat.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
