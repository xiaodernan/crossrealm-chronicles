## One Hundred and Ninety-eight: Azu's Notebook

The sixth week after the spring equinox — Monday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi was going through Azu's old notebook — the fire burned — the paper was yellow — the hand was narrow — he could read half — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — Azu wrote it — what he thought while tending the fire — is all in here —”

A-Shi said: “ — Azu — what did he write —”

His mother said: “ — keeping the fire — the kindling — step by step —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — mother said the kindling was — one piece of wood —”

His mother said: “ — the kindling — is not one piece — it is what is kept — it is what is passed on —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi turned to the last page — the fire burned — there was only one character on it — “守” — the staying — the keeping — the word was very old — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — 守 —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
