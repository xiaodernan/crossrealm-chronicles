## Two Hundred and Seventy-Seven: Frost's Descent

The fourteenth day after start of autumn — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — white on the ground — he said: “ — frost’s descent —” — his mother said: “ — frost’s descent —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — grass tips white as salt — freezing to the touch — he said: “ — frost’s descent has come —”; his mother said: “ — frost’s descent has come —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — ice flakes — he said: “ — it’s frozen —”; his mother said: “ — it’s frozen —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — winter is near —”; A-Shi said: “ — winter is near —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —”; A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Frost's descent.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
