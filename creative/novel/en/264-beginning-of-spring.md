## Two Hundred and Sixty-Four: Beginning of Spring

The first week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the wind had softened — he said: “ — spring begins —” — his mother said: “ — spring begins —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the ridge of the field — there were traces on the ground — small sprouts — he said: “ — the ground has come alive —” — his mother said: “ — the ground has come alive —” — the fire burned — after a while — the sun came out — it was a little warmer — A-Shi came back — he said: “ — it is a little warmer —” — his mother said: “ — it is a little warmer —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — a year has come full circle —” — A-Shi said: “ — a year has come full circle —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Beginning of spring.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
