## Two Hundred and Seventy-One: Summer Solstice

The thirteenth day after summer begins — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun was fiercest — the shadow was shortest — he said: “ — summer solstice —” — his mother said: “ — summer solstice —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the wheat ears hung low — grain by grain gold — the hand clasped hot wind — he said: “ — it is nearly ripe —” — his mother said: “ — it is nearly ripe —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi broke off an ear — he said: “ — this kernel is the fullest —” — his mother said: “ — this kernel is the fullest —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the longest day of the year —” — A-Shi said: “ — the longest day of the year —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Summer solstice.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
