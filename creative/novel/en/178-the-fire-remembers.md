## One Hundred and Seventy-eight: The Fire Remembers

The thirteenth day — late at night — the fire burned.

A-Shi hardly slept — he watched the fire — he heard something at the back of the house — not a sound — a stillness — he said: “Ma —”

His mother said: “It will remember.”

A-Shi said: “Remember what?”

His mother said: “You —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “Then will it remember me instead?”

His mother said: “ — It will.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
