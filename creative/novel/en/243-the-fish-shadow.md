## Two Hundred and Forty-Three: The Fish Shadow

The twenty-seventh week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — he looked at the riverbank — he said: “ — there is a shadow —” — his mother said: “ — there is a shadow —” — A-Shi made it out — it was a fish — it was swimming in the water — it swam past — he said: “ — the fish shadow —” — his mother said: “ — the fish shadow —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked again — a second one — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — two fish —” — his mother said: “ — two fish —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The fish shadow.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
