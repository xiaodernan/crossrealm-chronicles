## Two Hundred and Twenty-Seven: The Silo

The eleventh week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — A-Shi carried the ears in — the room could not hold them — he spread the ears across the floor — one row — two rows — three rows — A-Shi said: “ — the silo —” — his mother said: “ — the silo —” — there was sweat on A-Shi's forehead — his mother handed him a cup of water — he said: “ — it is a lot —” — his mother said: “ — it is a lot —” — the fire burned — he looked at the heap of ears — he said: “ — it is a great many —” — his mother said: “ — it is a great many —” — the fire burned — the ears in the room — the lamp lit — his mother watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The silo.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
