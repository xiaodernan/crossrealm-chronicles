## One Hundred and Thirty-eight: The Two Words He Said Aloud

Saturday of the twentieth week after the spring equinox — morning — the light crisp and bright.

Aunt Guo came again — Uncle Zhou came too — the whole family stood at the stove — the fire burned.

Mother carried three bowls of rice gruel from the kitchen — she set them on the table — she did not speak — the fire leapt once more.

No one spoke.

Uncle Zhou and Aunt Guo took their seats too — they both lifted a bowl — no one said a word.

The fire burned.

When they had finished — Xiao Huo went to the kitchen to wash the bowls — Mother went too — Aunt Guo stayed by the stove — she still did not speak — she watched the fire.

Uncle Zhou sat there too — he had not finished his bowl — he watched the fire — he did not speak either.

Mother brought the clean bowls out — she set them on the table — Aunt Guo raised her eyes to the fire once — she did not look at Mother.

A-Shi watched the fire — he knew — the two characters — he had found them.

“炉火。” A-Shi said.

No one moved — Uncle Zhou did not move — Aunt Guo did not move — Mother did not move — Xiao Huo did not move either — he was standing at the stove with a bowl in his hand.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
