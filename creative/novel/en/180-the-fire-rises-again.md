## One Hundred and Eighty: The Fire Rises Again

The first week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — just a sliver of light.

The fire burned.

A-Shi woke — the first thing he did was look at the stove — the fire burned.

His mother was not at the stove — she was in the room — she was looking — not moving — she listened to the fire burning.

A-Shi said: “Ma — what hundredth year is it?”

His mother said: “ — the seven-hundred-and-second —”

A-Shi said: “ — ”

His mother said: “ — if you can hold it, it stays the seven-hundred-and-second —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — good.”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
