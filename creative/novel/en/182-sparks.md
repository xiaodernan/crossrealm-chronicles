## One Hundred and Eighty-two: Sparks

The first week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — late morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi carried out from the room a small porcelain jar — he did not look — he set it on the table.

His mother said: “That is.”

A-Shi said: “ — sparks —”

His mother said: “Yes.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — this burns once — it leaves one —”

His mother said: “ — Yes.”

A-Shi said: “ — Azu kept it — for the seven-hundred-and-second —”

His mother said: “ — Yes — if you can stay —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
