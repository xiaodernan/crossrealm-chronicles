## Two Hundred and Fifty-Six: Cold Dew

The fortieth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — early morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the dew on the grass shone again — the wind had cooled — he said: “ — cold dew —” — his mother said: “ — cold dew —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — he looked at the grass — there was dew on it — it shone — the wind passed — the dew was gone — he said: “ — the dew is cold —” — his mother said: “ — the dew is cold —” — the fire burned — after a while — a sparrow called once — it flew away — southward — he said: “ — the sparrow has gone —” — his mother said: “ — the sparrow has gone —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the nights are long —” — A-Shi said: “ — the nights are long —” — his mother said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Cold dew.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
