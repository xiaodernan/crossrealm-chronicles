## Two Hundred and Eighty-Four: Spring Breeze

The fifth day after start of spring — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — ice strings hanging from the eaves — he said: " — spring breeze —"; his mother said: " — spring breeze —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — ice strings fully melted — he said: " — ice melts —"; his mother said: " — ice melts —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — clear water — he said: " — clear water —"; his mother said: " — clear water —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — renewal —"; A-Shi said: " — renewal —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Spring breeze.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
