## Two Hundred and Seventy-Nine: Heavy Snow

The twenty-second day after start of autumn — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — white thick on the ground — he said: " — heavy snow —"; his mother said: " — heavy snow —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — grass tips white as frost — icy to the touch — he said: " — heavy snow has fallen —"; his mother said: " — heavy snow has fallen —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — water had frozen into ice — he said: " — water froze —"; his mother said: " — water froze —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — winter has arrived —"; A-Shi said: " — winter has arrived —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Heavy snow.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
