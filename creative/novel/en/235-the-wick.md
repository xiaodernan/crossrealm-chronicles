## Two Hundred and Thirty-Five: The Wick

The nineteenth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the night had deepened — inside the hearth — the fire burned — she trimmed the wick — the lamp brightened — A-Shi said: “ — trim the wick —” — his mother said: “ — trim the wick —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked at it — the lamp grew brighter — he said: “ — the wick —” — his mother said: “ — the wick —” — A-Shi watched — the fire burned — his mother looked at him — he said: “ — I'm going to read —” — his mother said: “ — you're going to read —” — the fire burned — the lamp lit — A-Shi read — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The wick.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
