## One Hundred and Forty-six: Tomorrow, Then

Monday of the twenty-fifth week after the spring equinox — morning — the sky very grey.

Aunt Guo had said "tomorrow" — she came today — she brought one thing — she set the cards on the table — she turned to leave.

A-Shi stopped her — "You’re going?"

Aunt Guo stopped — she did not turn around — she said nothing.

A-Shi chased her out — she was already at the gate — it was bright outside the gate — he caught up with her —

"Can’t we say it today?" he asked.

Aunt Guo finally turned around — she looked at him — she said nothing — he saw the stove in the direction of the kitchen — he walked back toward it.

He turned around — Aunt Guo turned too — the two of them walked back together.

His mother stood at the stove — the fire burned — no one looked up — the fire burned.

A-Shi crouched — he held his two hands — he did not speak — his mother said nothing either.

The fire burned.

Aunt Guo said: “Everyone sit.” His mother sat — A-Shi sat too.

Aunt Guo said: “We say it today.” She laid the cards on the table — she did not look at the fire — she only looked at the two of them —

“The seventh card — you’ve already learned it.”

A-Shi did not lift his head — his mother did not move — the fire burned.

Aunt Guo said again: “Say it.”

A-Shi still did not lift his head — he looked at his own hands — he closed them — he opened them again.

He spoke one character slowly.

His mother looked at him — Aunt Guo saw it too — the fire burned.

Aunt Guo nodded — “That’s it.”

The fire burned.

Aunt Guo said: “This character — fire — it burned for seven hundred years.”

A-Shi did not answer — the fire burned.

Aunt Guo gathered the few cards — she left — she said nothing — she went through the door.

The fire burned.

Aunt Guo had gone — A-Shi crouched at the stove — his mother sat at the stove — the fire burned.

His mother patted his shoulder — she did not speak — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
