## Two Hundred and Sixty-One: Winter Solstice

The forty-fifth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun rose at its latest hour — the night was at its longest — he said: “ — winter solstice —” — his mother said: “ — winter solstice —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — he looked at the sky — the sun had just risen — the light was faint — he said: “ — the night is at its longest —” — his mother said: “ — the night is at its longest —” — the fire burned — his mother fed one more stick into the stove — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — from today onward —” — A-Shi said: “ — from today onward —” — his mother said: “ — the days will grow longer —” — A-Shi said: “ — the days will grow longer —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Winter solstice.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
