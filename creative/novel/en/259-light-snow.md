## Two Hundred and Fifty-Nine: Light Snow

The forty-third week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sky had been gray for a whole day — he said: “ — it is going to snow —” — his mother said: “ — it is going to snow —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he looked up — the clouds had thickened — he said: “ — it is coming —” — his mother said: “ — it is coming —” — the fire burned — after a while — grains of snow began to fall — they made no sound — but you could see them — he said: “ — light snow —” — his mother said: “ — light snow —” — the fire burned — A-Shi reached out — the grains of snow fell into his palm — they melted — he said: “ — it is cold —” — his mother said: “ — it is cold —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — winter has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — winter has deepened —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Light snow.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
