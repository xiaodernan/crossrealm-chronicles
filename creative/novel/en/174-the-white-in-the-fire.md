## One Hundred and Seventy-four: The White in the Fire

The ninth day — before first light — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — he watched the fire — occasionally a small white figure rose in it — like a birch —

A-Shi said softly: “Azu —”

The fire burned.

The air was quiet — from the side of the Greywood — no sound — the fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

His mother pushed the door open — holding the warm water — she said: “ — he comes back often —”

A-Shi nodded — he did not speak — the fire burned.

His mother put the warm water in his hand — she said: “The white — is not asking you to find anyone — the white — is left for you to see —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
