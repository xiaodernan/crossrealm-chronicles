## Two Hundred and Fifty-One: Minor Heat

The thirty-fifth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — noon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the noon sun — the heat — he said: “ — minor heat —” — his mother said: “ — minor heat —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the well — the well water was cool — he said: “ — the well water is cool —” — his mother said: “ — the well water is cool —” — the fire burned — A-Shi drew a jug of water — he came back — he said: “ — the water is cool —” — his mother said: “ — the water is cool —” — the fire burned — after a while — a wave of hot wind passed through and then stopped — A-Shi soaked his handkerchief in the water — he draped it over his head — he said: “ — a little cover —” — his mother said: “ — a little cover —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Minor heat.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
