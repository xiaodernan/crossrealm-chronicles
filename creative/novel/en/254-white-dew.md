## Two Hundred and Fifty-Four: White Dew

The thirty-eighth week after the spring equinox — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was dew on the grass — it shone — he said: “ — white dew —” — his mother said: “ — white dew —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked to the edge of the grass — he crouched down — he reached out — the dew clung to his fingertips — he said: “ — it is cool —” — his mother said: “ — it is cool —” — the fire burned — after a while — the sun came out — the dew dried — the grass was no longer shining — he said: “ — the dew has dried —” — his mother said: “ — the dew has dried —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — white dew has come —” — A-Shi said: “ — white dew has come —” — his mother said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

White dew.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
