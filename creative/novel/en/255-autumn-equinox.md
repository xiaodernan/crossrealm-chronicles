## Two Hundred and Fifty-Five: Autumn Equinox

The thirty-ninth week after the spring equinox — Monday — early morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun came out right on time — day and night were of the same length — he said: “ — autumn equinox —” — his mother said: “ — autumn equinox —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — he looked toward the east — the sun had just appeared — half the sky was lit — he said: “ — day and night are the same —” — his mother said: “ — day and night are the same —” — the fire burned — after a while — the wind was cool — a bright ring formed on the paper of the window — he said: “ — the wind is cool —” — his mother said: “ — the wind is cool —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the nights are as long now —” — A-Shi said: “ — the nights are as long now —” — his mother said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — autumn has deepened —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Autumn equinox.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
