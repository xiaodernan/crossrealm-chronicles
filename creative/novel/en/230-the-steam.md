## Two Hundred and Thirty: The Steam

The fourteenth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — his mother was already at the fire — the water had boiled — she placed the buns into the steamer — she covered it — the fire burned — A-Shi stood beside her — he smelled — there was the scent of dough — the fire burned — his mother watched the pot — she said: “ — wait —” — he said: “ — wait —” — his mother said: “ — wait —” — the fire burned — after a while — she lifted the lid — she said: “ — ready —” — A-Shi said: “ — ready —” — the fire burned — the buns came out of the steamer — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The steam.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
