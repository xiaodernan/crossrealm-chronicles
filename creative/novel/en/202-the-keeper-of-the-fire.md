## Two Hundred and Two: The Keeper of the Fire

The eighth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood before leaving — the fire burned — his mother looked at him — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — go cut wood —”

A-Shi said: “ — when the wood is small — the flame burns well —”

His mother said: “ — a keeper of the fire — must know wood — the fire does not want the biggest piece — but the right one —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — the right one —”

His mother said: “ — the right one —”

The fire burned.

The keeper of the fire.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
