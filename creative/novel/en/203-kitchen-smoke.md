## Two Hundred and Three: Kitchen Smoke

The eighth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi lit the stove — the fire burned — the flame dimmed — he did not turn on the lamp — the fire burned.

He sat before the stove — the fire burned — his mother was washing bowls in the other room — the fire burned.

The smoke curled up — he watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — the north wind is coming — the smoke will lean west —”

A-Shi said: “ — the smoke will lean west —”

His mother said: “ — the smoke of the hearth — each day it leans in one direction —”

The fire burned.

Kitchen smoke.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
