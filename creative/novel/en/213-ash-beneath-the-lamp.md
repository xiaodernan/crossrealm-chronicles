## Two Hundred and Thirteen: Ash Beneath the Lamp

The first week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi lit the lamp — the fire burned — the lamp showed the stove's mouth — the fire burned — ash fell onto his hand — he watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — ash on the palm — the hand does not shake —”

A-Shi said: “ — it does not shake —”

His mother said: “ — the keeper's hand —”

The fire burned.

Ash beneath the lamp.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
