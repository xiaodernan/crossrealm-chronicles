## One Hundred and Eighty-one: The Fire Did Not Say

The first week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — late morning — the sunlight stayed for a while.

The fire burned.

A-Shi was boiling water — the water did not ask — he said: “The way — what is the way —”

His mother was in the room — she did not speak — she only listened.

A-Shi said: “ — if you don’t say it — I don’t know how to say it —”

His mother said: “ — it never says — if it said — you would know —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — the way — something you do — the way — something you don’t need to say —”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
