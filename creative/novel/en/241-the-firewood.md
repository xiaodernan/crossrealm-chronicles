## Two Hundred and Forty-One: The Firewood

The twenty-fifth week after the spring equinox — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the firewood pile was low — A-Shi said: “ — the firewood —” — his mother said: “ — the firewood —” — A-Shi went out — he picked up an armful of wood — he placed it into the hearth — the fire burned — A-Shi placed a second armful — the fire burned — he placed a third — the fire burned — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — enough —” — A-Shi said: “ — enough —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The firewood.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
