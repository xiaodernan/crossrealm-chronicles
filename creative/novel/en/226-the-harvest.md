## Two Hundred and Twenty-Six: The Harvest

The tenth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the ears had turned yellow — old ears — the wind blew — the wind passed — the stalks moved — he brought out the sickle — he said: “ — the harvest —” — his mother said: “ — the harvest —” — A-Shi walked out — he squatted down — he cut — the first stroke — the ears fell — the second stroke — the ears fell — the wind came again — he stopped — he watched — the fire burned — he said: “ — ah —” — his mother said: “ — ah —” — he cut again — the stalks fell — the fire burned — he carried the ears home — the fire burned.

The harvest.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
