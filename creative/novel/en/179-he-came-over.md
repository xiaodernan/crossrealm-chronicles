## One Hundred and Seventy-nine: He Came Over

The fourteenth day — before first light — the fire burned.

A-Shi could not sleep — he got up — he walked to the stove — he set the last piece of wood inside — it did not take — the fire burned.

He did not add more — he only stood there — the fire burned.

His mother came out of the room — she stood behind A-Shi — she saw the fire burned — she slowly came closer — she took his hand — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — he’s back —”

A-Shi said: “ — back? —”

His mother said: “If you can stay — he comes back —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
