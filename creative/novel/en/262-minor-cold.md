## Two Hundred and Sixty-Two: Minor Cold

The forty-sixth week after the spring equinox — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun came out late — the sky was a deep cold — he said: “ — minor cold —” — his mother said: “ — minor cold —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — there was a layer of frost on the ground — the ground had turned white — he said: “ — the ground is white —” — his mother said: “ — the ground is white —” — the fire burned — A-Shi reached out — the frost did not stick — he said: “ — the frost does not stick —” — his mother said: “ — the frost does not stick —” — the fire burned — after a while — the wind hardened — it grew a little colder — he said: “ — the wind is hard —” — his mother said: “ — the wind is hard —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — winter has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — winter has deepened —” — his mother said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — A-Shi said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Minor cold.
