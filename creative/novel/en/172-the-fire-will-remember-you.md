## One Hundred and Seventy-two: The Fire Will Remember You

The eighth day — late at night — the fire burned.

A-Shi couldn’t sleep — he wanted to keep the fire — he sat before the stove — he was not sure how long he could stay — he said: “Mother said — you can stay —”

The fire burned.

He put the charcoal back — he looked at it — its edges were whitening — he said: “It won’t wake —”

The fire burned.

His mother was in the room — she was not asleep — she said softly: “The fire will remember you — can you stay?”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

A-Shi said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
