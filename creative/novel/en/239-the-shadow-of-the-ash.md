## Two Hundred and Thirty-Nine: The Shadow of the Ash

The twenty-third week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fire burned — he took a handful of ash — he scattered it across the earthen floor — he said: “ — scatter —” — his mother said: “ — scatter —” — A-Shi finished scattering — a white film lay across the floor — he looked — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — the shadow —” — his mother said: “ — the shadow —” — A-Shi said: “ — the ash casts a shadow —” — his mother said: “ — the ash casts a shadow —” — the fire burned — A-Shi watched — the ash — the shadow — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The shadow of the ash.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
