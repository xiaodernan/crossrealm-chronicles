## Two Hundred and Sixty-Seven: Spring Equinox

The second week after spring begins — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun had come out — the sky had brightened — he said: “ — spring equinox —” — his mother said: “ — spring equinox —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the sun was rising — the shadow fell — the shadows were of equal length — he said: “ — the shadows are equally long —” — his mother said: “ — the shadows are equally long —” — the fire burned — after a while — the wind had softened — the flowers had opened — he said: “ — the flowers have opened —” — his mother said: “ — the flowers have opened —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked again — the grass had turned green — he said: “ — the grass is green —” — his mother said: “ — the grass is green —” — his mother said: “ — the year has come around —” — A-Shi said: “ — the year has come around —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Spring equinox.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
