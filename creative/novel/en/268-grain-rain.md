## Two Hundred and Sixty-Eight: Grain Rain

Twenty-four days after spring begins — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was rain in the sky — he said: “ — grain rain —” — his mother said: “ — grain rain —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the rain fell — fine and thin — the ground grew wet — he said: “ — the rain has fallen —” — his mother said: “ — the rain has fallen —” — the fire burned — after a while — the rain stopped — the sky had cleared — there was water on the ground — he said: “ — the rain has stopped —” — his mother said: “ — the rain has stopped —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the grain rain has fallen —” — A-Shi said: “ — the grain rain has fallen —” — his mother said: “ — the crops are about to grow —” — A-Shi said: “ — the crops are about to grow —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain rain.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
