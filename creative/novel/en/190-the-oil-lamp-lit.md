## One Hundred and Ninety: The Oil Lamp Lit

The third week after the spring equinox — Friday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the sky was fully dark — his mother set down two pieces of mantou — the fire burned.

He felt for the matches — the oil lamp lit — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — look —”

A-Shi said: “ — it is lit —”

His mother said: “ — Azu said — the last lamp — will be seen —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi sat beneath the lamp — he could not see Azu — he could see the flame — the fire burned — he said nothing else — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The oil lamp was lit.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
