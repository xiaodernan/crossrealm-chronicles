## Two Hundred and Forty-Four: The West Wind Rises

The twenty-eighth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the wind had risen — the wind came from the west — A-Shi said: “ — the west wind —” — his mother said: “ — the west wind —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he looked at the sky — the clouds were moving — he said: “ — the clouds are moving —” — his mother said: “ — the clouds are moving —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The west wind rises.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
