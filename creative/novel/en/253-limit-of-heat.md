## Two Hundred and Fifty-Three: Limit of Heat

The thirty-seventh week after the spring equinox — Saturday — early morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun came up late — the wind had cooled — he said: “ — limit of heat —” — his mother said: “ — limit of heat —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked behind the house — the persimmons were ripe — one — two — he said: “ — the persimmons are red —” — his mother said: “ — the persimmons are red —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked further back — the swallows were flying south — he said: “ — the swallows have gone —” — his mother said: “ — the swallows have gone —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — major heat has passed —” — A-Shi said: “ — major heat has passed —” — his mother said: “ — autumn draws near —” — A-Shi said: “ — autumn draws near —” — the fire burned — after a while — a V in the sky — another — he said: “ — another flock —” — his mother said: “ — another flock —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Limit of heat.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
