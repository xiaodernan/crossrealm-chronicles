## Two Hundred and Forty-Two: The Riverbank

The twenty-sixth week after the spring equinox — Monday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — he carried two bowls — he walked out — he walked to the river — he placed one bowl on the stone — he said: “ — the river —” — his mother said: “ — the river —” — A-Shi sat down — he looked at the river — the fire burned — he said: “ — the water —” — his mother said: “ — the water —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The riverbank.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
