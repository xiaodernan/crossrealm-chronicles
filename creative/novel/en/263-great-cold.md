## Two Hundred and Sixty-Three: Great Cold

The forty-seventh week after the spring equinox — Monday — early morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the sun rose late — the deep of the cold was at hand — he said: “ — great cold —” — his mother said: “ — great cold —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — a white layer of frost lay on the ground — hard bits of ice — he said: “ — the ground is iced —” — his mother said: “ — the ground is iced —” — the fire burned — he stepped on it once — the ice broke — he said: “ — the ice has broken —” — his mother said: “ — the ice has broken —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi came back — he carried an armful of firewood — he said: “ — winter has come to its limit —” — his mother said: “ — winter has come to its limit —” — his mother said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — A-Shi said: “ — don’t let the stove go out —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Great cold.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
