## Two Hundred and Fifty: Summer Solstice

The thirty-fourth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the day was long — the shadows of clouds moved slowly — he said: “ — summer solstice —” — his mother said: “ — summer solstice —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — he looked at the sky — the sun traveled from morning to evening — its wheel moved slowly — he said: “ — the longest day —” — his mother said: “ — the longest day —” — the fire burned — his mother called from the kitchen: “ — grain in ear has passed —” — A-Shi said: “ — grain in ear has passed —” — his mother said: “ — the solstice has come —” — A-Shi said: “ — the solstice has come —” — the fire burned — after a while — the clouds thinned a little — the wind fell still — the sun's shadow edged a little eastward — he said: “ — the sun is turning back —” — his mother said: “ — the sun is turning back —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Summer solstice.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
