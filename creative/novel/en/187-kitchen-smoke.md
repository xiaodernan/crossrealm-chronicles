## One Hundred and Eighty-seven: Kitchen Smoke

The third week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the birch stood a little taller than yesterday — his mother carried porridge — she said: “ — kitchen smoke —”

A-Shi said: “ — smoke —”

His mother said: “ — from the stove — out the door — up to the sky —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — who can see it —”

His mother said: “ — whoever still walks — can see it —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi stood by the door — he looked at the sky — the fire burned — one thread of smoke — rising straight and tall — the fire burned.

The smoke dispersed.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
