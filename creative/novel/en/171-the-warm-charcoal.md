## One Hundred and Seventy-one: The Warm Charcoal

The next day — before first light — his mother pushed the door open — holding a cup of warm water — she saw A-Shi sitting before the stove — the charcoal in his hand — it had cooled.

His mother said: “It isn’t spoiled —”

A-Shi said: “It has cooled.”

His mother said: “The charcoal cools — the fire doesn’t —”

She said she meant the small one in the stove — A-Shi turned his head — he saw the fire — it was very small — but he saw it — he said nothing.

His mother said: “Azu used to say — fire doesn’t choose the hand — it chooses the heart — it chooses who can stay —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi nodded — he said nothing — he put the charcoal back before the stove — he said softly: “I can stay.”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
