## Two Hundred and Eight: The Winter of the Vigil

The eleventh week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — there was frost on the window — the fire burned — he did not open the door — he looked at the fire first — the fire burned.

He added wood — the flame rose — the fire burned — his mother was making breakfast — she did not look up — she heard him get up — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — frost —”

His mother said: “ — the winter of the vigil will pass —”

A-Shi said: “ — it will pass —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi went outside — frost settled on his shoes — he did not shake — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.
