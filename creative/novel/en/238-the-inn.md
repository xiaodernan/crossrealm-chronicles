## Two Hundred and Thirty-Eight: The Inn

The twenty-second week after the spring equinox — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the west wind had stopped — the fire burned — he took up the broom — he swept from the door to the kitchen table — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — sweep —” — A-Shi said: “ — sweep —” — A-Shi finished the sweep — on the floor — there was a birch leaf — A-Shi bent down — he picked it up — he said: “ — a birch leaf —” — his mother said: “ — a birch leaf —” — A-Shi placed the birch leaf into the hearth — the fire burned — he said: “ — the birch went into the hearth —” — his mother said: “ — the birch went into the hearth —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The inn.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
