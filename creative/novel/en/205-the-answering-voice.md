## Two Hundred and Five: The Answering Voice

The ninth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi split wood — the axe fell — the wood split — he heard a dull sound — the fire burned — as if someone in the room had said something — but very soft — the fire burned — A-Shi smiled — the fire burned.

He said: “ — hey —”

The fire burned.

He waited a while — the wind grew softer — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — who are you talking to —”

A-Shi said: “ — the fire —”

His mother said: “ — it will answer you — but only if you have not lied to it —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I have not lied to it —”

His mother said: “ — then it will answer —”

The fire burned.

The answering voice.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
