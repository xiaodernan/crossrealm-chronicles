## Two Hundred and Thirty-One: The Steam Heat

The fifteenth week after the spring equinox — Sunday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the steamed buns were still hot — he took one up — he took a bite — it was scalding — he let out a breath — the fire burned — he said: “ — hot —” — his mother said: “ — hot —” — A-Shi held the bun in his hands — he waited a moment — he took another bite — it was just right — the fire burned — his mother watched — he said: “ — the steam heat —” — his mother said: “ — the steam heat —” — he split the bun in two — he gave half to his mother — his mother took it — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The steam heat.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
