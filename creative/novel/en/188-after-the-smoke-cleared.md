## One Hundred and Eighty-eight: After the Smoke Cleared

The third week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was no smoke in the sky — the birch swayed in the wind — the fire burned.

His mother handed him the bowl — her hands were empty — she said: “ — the kitchen smoke is gone —”

A-Shi said: “ — gone —”

His mother said: “ — gone — it’s gone —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi looked at the sky — it was very high — the fire burned — he thought — no one had told him to leave — no one had told him to stay — the fire burned.

He stayed.

The smoke cleared.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
