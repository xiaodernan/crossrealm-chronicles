## Two Hundred and Sixty: Great Snow

The forty-fourth week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the snow had fallen from the sky — it had turned the ground white — he said: “ — great snow —” — his mother said: “ — great snow —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the snow lay on the ground — a white layer of it — he said: “ — the ground is white —” — his mother said: “ — the ground is white —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked at the tree — snow rested on it too — he said: “ — the tree is white —” — his mother said: “ — the tree is white —” — the fire burned — after a while — the snow grew heavier — flake by flake — he said: “ — the snow is heavier —” — his mother said: “ — the snow is heavier —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — winter has deepened —” — A-Shi said: “ — winter has deepened —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Great snow.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
