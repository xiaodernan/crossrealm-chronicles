## Two Hundred and Six: The Night of the Vigil

The tenth week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi did not sleep all night — he kept the vigil — the fire burned — he did not light the oil lamp — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The sky brightened.

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I kept it —”

His mother said: “ — you kept it —”

The fire burned.

The night of the vigil.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
