## One Hundred and Sixty-eight: The First Log

The third day — morning — A-Shi got up alone.

He walked to the yard — he saw his mother had left a bundle of logs by the stone block — he stood there and looked for a long time.

The fire burned.

He walked over — he picked one up — one that was not thick — and not thin either — he walked to the stove — he crouched down — and laid it inside.

The fire did not burst — it simply burned a little brighter — steadily — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove for a long time — he listened to the sound of the wood in the fire — it crackled — it did not speak — it only burned.

The fire burned.

His mother came out from the room — she stood at the door — she did not come in — she looked at A-Shi for a while — she said nothing — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
