## Two Hundred and Seventy-Four: White Dew

The fourth day after start of autumn — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — white beads on the grass — he said: “ — white dew —” — his mother said: “ — white dew —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — grass tips gleamed crystal — a touch of cool gathered them — he said: “ — white dew has fallen —” — his mother said: “ — white dew has fallen —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — icy to the bone — he said: “ — cold water —” — his mother said: “ — cold water —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — autumn is growing —” — A-Shi said: “ — autumn is growing —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

White dew.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
