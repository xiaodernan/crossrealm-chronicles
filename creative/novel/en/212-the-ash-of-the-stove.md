## Two Hundred and Twelve: The Ash of the Stove

The first week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi swept the stove — the ash was fine — the fire burned — he gathered the ash into a pan — he watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

He did not know — the fire burned — where to pour the ash — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — into the stove chamber —” — A-Shi said: “ — into the stove chamber —” — his mother said: “ — into the stove chamber —” — the fire burned.

The ash of the stove.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
