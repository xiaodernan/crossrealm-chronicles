## Two Hundred and Eighteen: Summer Fire

The second week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the birch leaves were fully out — twice the size of a thumb — the wind was light — sunlight came out — the fire burned — he watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — summer fire —”

A-Shi said: “ — summer fire —”

His mother said: “ — the fire must be kept —”

A-Shi said: “ — the fire must be kept —”

The fire burned.

Summer fire.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
