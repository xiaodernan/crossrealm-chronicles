## Two Hundred: Mending the Hearth

The seventh week after the spring equinox — Wednesday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi split wood — half of it done — he watched — the fire burned — the flame steadier — it would not go out — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — mend the hearth —”

A-Shi said: “ — mend the hearth —”

His mother said: “ — the hearth needs fixing — a crack in the body of the stove —”

A-Shi said: “ — mend it —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi took clay — he sealed the crack — the fire burned — the flame steadied — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — it is old — but it will not break —”

A-Shi said: “ — it will not break —”

The fire burned.

Mending the hearth.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
