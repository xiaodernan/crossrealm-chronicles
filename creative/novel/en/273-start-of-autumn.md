## Two Hundred and Seventy-Three: Start of Autumn

The thirtieth day after summer begins — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there was a chill in the wind — he said: “ — start of autumn —” — his mother said: “ — start of autumn —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the treetop leaves had a tinge of yellow — he said: “ — the leaves are turning yellow —” — his mother said: “ — the leaves are turning yellow —” — the fire burned — after a while — geese called in the distance — the sky had grown high — he said: “ — the geese are returning —” — his mother said: “ — the geese are returning —” — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — nights will turn cool —” — A-Shi said: “ — nights will turn cool —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Start of autumn.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
