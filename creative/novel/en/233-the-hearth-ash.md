## Two Hundred and Thirty-Three: The Hearth Ash

The seventeenth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the fire was low — in the belly of the hearth — the ash had thickened — he said: “ — it is time to sweep —” — his mother said: “ — it is time to sweep —” — A-Shi took a broom — he swept through it — the ash was less — his mother watched — she said: “ — it is clean —” — A-Shi said: “ — it is clean —” — the fire burned — A-Shi swept again — inside the belly of the hearth — the ash was gone — he said: “ — it is done —” — his mother said: “ — it is done —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The hearth ash.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
