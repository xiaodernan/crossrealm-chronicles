## One Hundred and Ninety-six: Firewood

The fifth week after the spring equinox — Friday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi split firewood — his axe was steady — one piece per swing — the fire burned — the wood split — he smelled the wood — pine — the fire burned.

His mother came in from the courtyard — she said: “ — Azu split this kind —”

A-Shi said: “ — Azu —”

His mother said: “ — when Azu split the wood — the fire never went out —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi split another piece — the fire burned — the wood became more orderly piece by piece — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother stacked the wood in the woodshed — the fire burned — she turned and said: “ — every piece is the fire's life —”

A-Shi said: “ — I remember —”

The fire burned.

Firewood.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
