## One Hundred and Ninety-one: By the Lamp

The third week after the spring equinox — Saturday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat by the lamp — his mother had fallen asleep — the fire burned — he did not add wood — the flame was right — the fire burned.

He turned his face — the lamp lit his hands — the fire burned — his hands were steady — the fire burned.

The lamp flickered — and lit again — the fire burned — nothing was wrong with the oil — it was the wind — the fire burned.

A-Shi did not turn it off — the fire burned — he thought — seven hundred years the fire has been kept — they kept it to the seven-hundred-and-second year — they kept it to his hands — the fire burned.

He did not sleep.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

By the lamp — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
