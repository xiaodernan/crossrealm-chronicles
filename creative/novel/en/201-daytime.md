## Two Hundred and One: Daytime

The seventh week after the spring equinox — Saturday — noon — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke — the light outside was already full — the fire burned — it was quiet — enough for now — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — Azu used to keep the fire in the day too —”

A-Shi said: “ — in the day — whom did he keep it for —”

His mother said: “ — he kept it for the afternoon —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — kept it for the afternoon —”

His mother said: “ — the fire doesn’t know — but someone must —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I am here —”

His mother said: “ — I am here —”

The fire burned.

Daytime.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
