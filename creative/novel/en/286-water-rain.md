## Two Hundred and Eighty-Six: Water Rain

The sixth day after start of spring — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — wet ground — he said: " — water rain —"; his mother said: " — water rain —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — wet ground — he said: " — wet ground —"; his mother said: " — wet ground —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — warm water — he said: " — warm water —"; his mother said: " — warm water —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — spring plowing begins —"; A-Shi said: " — spring plowing begins —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Water rain.
