## One Hundred and Ninety-seven: The Kindling

The fifth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi split a whole bundle of firewood — the fire burned — his mother said: “ — the kindling Azu left — is in this bundle of wood you carry in your memory —”

A-Shi said: “ — the kindling —”

His mother said: “ — the kindling —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I am burning it — it is the kindling —”

His mother said: “ — it is the kindling —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the fire burned — his mother sat beside him — the fire burned — one piece of wood after another — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

No one from outside would know — the fire burned — this bundle of wood — the fire burned — is the kindling of the seven-hundred-and-second year.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
