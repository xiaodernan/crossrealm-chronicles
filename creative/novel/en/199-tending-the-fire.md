## One Hundred and Ninety-nine: Tending the Fire

The sixth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi woke early — the fire burned — his mother was still asleep — the fire burned — the fire burned.

He added a small piece of wood to the fire — the wood was small — the flame was quiet — the fire burned — the fire burned.

He watched — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

His mother woke — she said: “ — tend the fire —”

A-Shi said: “ — tend the fire —”

His mother said: “ — tend the fire —”

The fire burned.

Tend the fire.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
