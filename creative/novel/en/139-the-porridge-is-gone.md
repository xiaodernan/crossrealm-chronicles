## One Hundred and Thirty-nine: The Porridge Is Gone

Saturday of the twenty-first week after the spring equinox — afternoon — wind rose against the courtyard.

A-Shi came home from outside — he rushed to the stove — the whole family was sitting there — the fire burned.

On the table — it was empty.

Mother was looking at the stove — she did not speak.

A-Shi looked at the white porcelain bowl — it stood at the corner of the table — he looked back at the fire — the fire burned.

Mother spoke — her voice was very low — she said: “The fire set it for us” — she did not move.

A-Shi did not move — Uncle Zhou did not move — Aunt Guo did not move — Xiao Huo did not move either.

Mother turned the white bowl over — the bottom was empty — she studied it a moment — she put it back.

No one said anything.

Aunt Guo finally spoke — her voice was very light — “It is full — it is open now.”

Uncle Zhou cleared his throat — he did not speak — he still kept silent.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
