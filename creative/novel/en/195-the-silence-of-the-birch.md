## One Hundred and Ninety-five: The Silence of the Birch

The fourth week after the spring equinox — Thursday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat by the lamp — the fire burned — the birch's shadow leaned on the window — the wind softened — the fire burned.

His mother said: “ — on to the next volume —”

A-Shi said: “ — the birch has no next volume —”

His mother said: “ — the birch's next volume lives in everything you keep —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I keep the fire —”

His mother said: “ — you keep the fire —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not add wood — the oil lamp flickered — and lit again — the fire burned.

The sky was nearly bright.

The fire burned.

The silence of the birch.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
