## Two Hundred and Eighty-Five: Rain

The second day after start of spring — Thursday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — roof sputtering — he said: " — rain —"; his mother said: " — rain —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — roof leaking — he said: " — water leaks —"; his mother said: " — water leaks —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — cool water — he said: " — cool water —"; his mother said: " — cool water —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — moistening silently —"; A-Shi said: " — moistening silently —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Rain.
