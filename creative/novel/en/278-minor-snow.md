## Two Hundred and Seventy-Eight: Minor Snow

The nineteenth day after start of autumn — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — white frost on the ground — he said: " — minor snow —"; his mother said: " — minor snow —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — grass tips white as frost — icy to the touch — he said: " — minor snow has fallen —"; his mother said: " — minor snow has fallen —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — water froze — he said: " — water froze —"; his mother said: " — water froze —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — footsteps of winter —"; A-Shi said: " — footsteps of winter —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Minor snow.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
