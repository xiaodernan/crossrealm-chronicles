## One Hundred and Sixty-two: The Sound of the Fire

Monday of the twenty-seventh week after the spring equinox — evening — the sky fully dark.

The fire burned — it made a very faint sound — very faint — almost too quiet to hear.

A-Shi said: “It’s talking.”

His mother sat beside him — she did not look — she only listened.

After a while, A-Shi said: “What is it saying?”

His mother said: “It says it is tired.”

A-Shi said: “Tired?”

His mother nodded — she said: “Seven hundred years — it has never stopped — it is tired too.”

A-Shi looked at the fire — the fire burned.

His mother said: “But it won’t stop — if it stopped, the fire would be gone.”

A-Shi said: “So we feed it?”

His mother nodded — she did not answer — she just looked at the fire for a moment.

The fire burned.

The two of them listened — neither spoke again.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
