## Two Hundred and Thirty-Two: The Split

The sixteenth week after the spring equinox — Monday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the steamed buns were still hot — A-Shi took one up — he looked — he saw the shell of the bun — the shell was cracked — it had split along one seam — he said: “ — the shell split —” — his mother said: “ — the shell split —” — A-Shi pulled the bun apart — it came apart — inside — it was white — he gave half to his mother — the fire burned — his mother took it — she looked at it — she said: “ — it tastes good —” — A-Shi said: “ — it tastes good —” — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The shell split.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
