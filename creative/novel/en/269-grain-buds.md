## Two Hundred and Sixty-Nine: Grain Buds

The fifth day after summer begins — Wednesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — there were clouds in the sky — white clouds — he said: “ — grain buds —” — his mother said: “ — grain buds —” — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — the wheat ears were filled — but not yet full — when the wind passed — the ears swayed — he said: “ — the wheat ears are filled —” — his mother said: “ — the wheat ears are filled —” — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi crouched down — he looked at the wheat waves — he said: “ — not yet full — it needs great fullness —” — his mother said: “ — not yet full — it needs great fullness —” — the fire burned — A-Shi looked again — a little farther — there was green at the foot of the hill — and water by the fields — he said: “ — there is water by the fields —” — his mother said: “ — there is water by the fields —” — his mother said: “ — filled but not full — that is the soundest state —” — A-Shi said: “ — filled but not full — that is the soundest state —” — his mother said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — A-Shi said: “ — the stove is still bright —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Grain buds.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
