## One Hundred and Forty-four: The Seventh Silent Day

Saturday of the twenty-fifth week after the spring equinox — afternoon — the sky very quiet.

A full week had passed — A-Shi had already learned the sixth card.

The sixth card — the white character — was “休” — he said it aloud — the fire burned.

His mother sat at the stove — she was crouching too — there was no tea at her side — she only watched the fire.

Aunt Guo went out — she went beyond the door — A-Shi did not move — the fire burned.

His mother did not ask where she had gone — the fire burned.

A-Shi picked up that card again — he looked at it — he looked for a long time.

His mother looked at him — “Can you keep it?” she asked.

“I can,” A-Shi said.

His mother did not reply — she watched the fire — the fire burned.

Toward evening — Aunt Guo came back — she came in — the fire burned — she said only: “It’s time for the next one.”

A-Shi nodded — his mother said nothing — the fire burned.

Aunt Guo spread the cards on the table — she counted them one by one — she counted up to the seventh — the fire burned.

The fire burned.
