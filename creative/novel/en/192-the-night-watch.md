## One Hundred and Ninety-two: The Night Watch

The fourth week after the spring equinox — Monday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat by the lamp — the lamp was lit — the fire burned — he memorized the shape of the flame — the fire burned.

Three in the morning — his mother had not woken — the fire burned — he added one piece of wood — it was small — he did not add more — the fire burned.

He heard, far away — the birch swaying in the wind — the fire burned — he did not need to look — the fire burned.

The east grew a thread of light — the lamp flickered — the fire burned — he added no more wood — the fire burned.

The sky brightened.

The fire burned.

By the night watch — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
