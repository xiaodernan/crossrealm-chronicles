## Two Hundred and Twenty-Five: The Wheat Sea

The ninth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — the wind came from the fields — the wind rose — the wind passed — all the ears moved — the ears were like water — like a sea — he stopped — he watched — the wind came — all the ears moved — the wind passed — all the ears moved — the fire burned — he said: “ — the wheat sea —” — his mother said: “ — the wheat sea —” — the wind came again — the ears swayed — he said: “ — the same —” — his mother said: “ — the same —” — the fire burned — the ears moved — the ears moved — the grains in their sheaths — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The wheat sea.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
