## One Hundred and Eighty-nine: The Last Lamp in the Stove-House

The third week after the spring equinox — Thursday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat before the stove — the oil lamp was not lit — it was too early — the sky was not yet fully dark — the fire burned.

His mother set the bowls down — she did not speak — she sat by the door — she did not look at him — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — what did Azu say —”

His mother said: “ — the last lamp in the stove-house —”

A-Shi said: “ — the last one — or the first —”

His mother said: “ — depending on whether you can stay and keep it —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I will stay —”

His mother said: “ — then — it is the first one —”

The fire burned.

A-Shi did not speak — the fire burned — the sky grew darker — the oil lamp flickered — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
