## Two Hundred and Eighty-One: Minor Cold

The twenty-eighth day after start of autumn — Saturday — morning — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — thin ice on the ground — he said: " — minor cold —"; his mother said: " — minor cold —" — the fire burned — A-Shi went out — he walked into the yard — thin ice cracked — he said: " — ice cracked —"; his mother said: " — ice cracked —" — the fire burned — after a while — A-Shi cupped a handful — warm water — he said: " — warm water —"; his mother said: " — warm water —" — the fire burned — his mother said: " — severe winter is approaching —"; A-Shi said: " — severe winter is approaching —"; his mother said: " — the stove is still bright —"; A-Shi said: " — the stove is still bright —" — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

Minor cold.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
