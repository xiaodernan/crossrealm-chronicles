## One Hundred and Seventy-seven: The Night in the Kitchen

The twelfth day — late at night — the fire burned.

A-Shi couldn’t sleep — he sat before the stove — his mother did not speak — she only sat across — she did not look at him — she watched the fire.

A-Shi said: “Ma —”

His mother did not answer.

A-Shi said: “Will the stove mind an extra person?”

His mother said: “It doesn’t.”

The fire burned.

A-Shi said: “Then I’ll sit with it a while longer.”

His mother said: “— All right.”

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
