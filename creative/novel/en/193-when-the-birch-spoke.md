## One Hundred and Ninety-three: When the Birch Spoke

The fourth week after the spring equinox — Tuesday — evening — the fire burned.

A-Shi sat by the lamp — the wind came from the birch — the fire burned — the birch leaves murmured in the wind — he listened — the fire burned.

He could not understand — the fire burned — but he nodded — the fire burned.

His mother slept — the fire burned — the fire burned — the birch went on murmuring — the fire burned — the flame stood straight — the fire burned.

A-Shi said: “ — I hear it —”

The fire burned.

The birch did not speak anymore.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

By the lamp — the fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
