## Two Hundred and Forty: The Ash Letter

The twenty-fourth week after the spring equinox — Saturday — afternoon — the fire burned.

A-Shi stood at the doorway — he took out an old piece of paper — he placed it on the table — he said: “ — look —” — his mother said: “ — look —” — A-Shi looked — there were characters on the page — the characters were faint — A-Shi said: “ — they have faded —” — his mother said: “ — they have faded —” — the fire burned — A-Shi said: “ — what does it say —” — his mother said: “ — what does it say —” — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned — the fire burned.

The ash letter.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.

The fire burned.
