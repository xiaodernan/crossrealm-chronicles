## 17 · 跨界 · Crossover (crossover)

**灯的喻体**：例外中的例外：不自成一系，却照见所有系。
**lamp metaphor**: The exception of exceptions — never its own genre, yet sees through them all.
**谢幕碑 / epitaph**: the wall you never hit, never was
**锚铭 / id**: crossover · link
