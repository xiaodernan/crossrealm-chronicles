# The Keeper’s Day (The Companion Short Film)

**Format**: Seven-minute short film · IMAX companion (crossrealm-chronicles end-coda short)
**Corresponds to**: Interlude II · The Keeper’s Reply (the other side of the fog) / Interlude III · Lin’s Reply to the Keeper (the closing letter) / OST 25 *Ember Low Hum* (only this frequency is used throughout)
**Rating**: G (no combat, no captions outside, almost no dialogue)
**Tagline**: Between the press of E and one inch of receding fog, someone is always keeping the light.

---

## Scene 1 · Interior · The Anchor · Dawn

**FADE IN:**

A wall of mist. No visible door.
There is only one thing in the fog — a small lamp hung in mid-air, not quite hanging, more like held by the fog itself.

The Keeper’s hands enter the frame. He doesn’t touch the fire; he only shakes the kindling.
Three sparks burst from the splitting wood — none of them reaches the ground — they rise, and scatter into the mist.

**The Keeper’s voice (only once):**

“One more today, who pressed E.”

The camera holds its distance. The fog parts by half an inch.

---

## Scene 2 · Loop montage · Silent

Close-ups of three first presses of **E**:

- First E: a nameless boy pressing his palm to the pillar of light at the anchor. No spark, no flame — skin against the pillar, no charge yet.
- Second E: a middle-aged man who has just finished composing a letter, fingertips resting on a fragment of an untitled letter on the hearth, unable to look up.
- Third E: a nameless passer-by, standing in front of the bulletin board for a long while, then walks on — pressed nothing.
  The Keeper sees him: **the one he pressed — somewhere inside.**

Every frame is without sound.
The only thing present is **41.2 Hz infrasonic**, sliding in as each trail cuts to another — the same band as OST 25.

---

## Scene 3 · Interior · The Anchor · Day

A traveler presses **F** — the camera is nearly immobile.

The traveler’s hand hangs one inch above the key.

**And the only thing that lights up in the frame is the Keeper’s toe.**

One spark pops from the toe. Just one. Then it goes out.

Fog yields another inch.

**The Keeper (not looking at the traveler, speaking to the fog):**

“He finished.”

The flame rhythm shifts a beat.
On the anchor point, a small new light appears — barely a full second before it registers as light, and later, like a letter rising from below.

---

## Scene 4 · Close-up · the Keeper’s desk · forever 11:06

Two things now fill the frame: the anchor flame, and a small thin volume open on the table.

The Keeper takes up a brush and writes down today’s few lines of accounts:

> Today there was no deep-one, no shallow-one.
> Today one came who had never been here — he pressed E, but he remembers every inch of fog.

He turns the page. The page turn is the only audible mechanical sound in the whole short.

**SFX:** A page — as though water-hardened.

---

## Scene 5 · Exterior · The Wasteland · Forever Night

Cut to outside. Day and night wheel in fast-forward 30× — only one thing moves:

**Every fixed interval, a faint gold flash pulses once deep in the fog.**

No one sees what that light is.
There is no need to see it.

Each pulse, the camera holds only 1.2 seconds, then black.
Seventeen pulses in total across the fast-forward.

During the 17th pulse, black card cuts to caption:

**FIN.**

---

## Scene 6 · Interior · The Anchor · Dusk

Back to the anchor. The fog remains, the lamp remains, the Keeper is still the Keeper.

This time, one colour in the flame is different — a colour from another plane (silver-blue), unassigned, unspecified;
for the audience, all we need to see is that it is *new*.

The Keeper shuts the stove window.

In the moment it closes, a very low hum escapes from the stove —

**E2 → A2, triangular wave. Slow. Passing.**

The final line of credits (slow fade, no score):

**When you turn around, I turn on.**

**END.**

## Addendum · Where this film sits in the cycle

The 142-minute film is “how the flame becomes the hearth.”
Interlude II tells “why the hearth never blows itself out.”
Interlude III tells “where the fire went after leaving the hearth.”

**This short, _The Keeper’s Day_, tells one thing only: the keeper of the hearth is still there today.**

No ending is added. Only a premise.
The crack in the last coda of the main film can be prepared in the last 30 seconds of this film — when the lamp dawns, the distant shot is given an extra five millimetres of framing.

*That crack is the final caption of this short.*
