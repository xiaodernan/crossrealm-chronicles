# 《跨界纪事》 · The Crossrealm Chronicles

**Title**: Crossrealm Chronicles  
**Format**: IMAX live-action · 3D · 142 minutes  
**Rating**: PG-13 (uncountable death-count / abstract violence)  
**Visual palette**: Deakins-style grey fog · subtle ancient fractures · time-lapse decay · noir-ish cyber neon

## Act One — The Beginning (0:00–0:38)

### SCENE 1 · EXT. PEAK · THE ANCHOR · WINTER EVENING

**FADE IN:**

The world seen from high above — a peak of stone, unmarked.  
The anchor — the sublime — **the world Anchor** — pulses warmly in the scenery outside the edge of the frame.

**KEEPER (V.O.)**:  
Fire doesn’t go out. You just have to keep watching for it.

**HIM**: 20 years old, well-built, a face the wind has given up on. Behind his back, the HUD fades in — and then he’s holding **the quest log entry: “The Anchor is kindling.”**

### SCENE 2 · INT. HUB · VILLAGE · EVENING

The Village of hollow saints is silent. He approaches — and **the Shadow Warden** is here, at this precise **TIME DROPS 40%** — and the taper of the candle is an interrogation.

**KEEPER (O.S.)**: Don’t get greedy.

**HIM**: (quietly) Q and Shift+Q lock-on, I know.

### SCENE 3 · EXT. ASH FOREST · EVENING · SNOW

Three strokes into the dust:
**THE KEEPER**: the stone ring has fallen? That’s what happens when a boss cuts you down.

**HIM**: (F — parry):
*Slightest pause.*

**KEEPER**: I’ll wait. You’ll want to decide soon.

### SCENE 4 · INT. ANCHOR STONE · EVENING

He turns to the anchor stone.  
**PICTURE**: *One Realm to bind them all.*

**SFX**: page turning in a library — deep as a volcano.

### SCENE 5 · INT. ANCHOR STONE · BOOKSIDE BOSS · END OF ACT I

**SHADOW WARDEN**: Tissue?  
First hit — **PARRY** — then one strike at a time.

The shadow anchor fights like a human.

**"KEEPER"** — maybe the fire is speaking.

**THE END OF ACT ONE**  
**INTERTITLE**  
*Crossrealm Chronicles*

## Act Two — The Pillar (0:38–1:12)

### SCENE 6 · EXT. VILLAGE · THE KEEPER · DUSK

The campfire rises against the dusk.  
**KEEPER’S VOICE** — with the wind, with the church, between the two.

He’s watching the fire — a lightness man, listening to the storm, not breaking any glass.

### SCENE 7 · INT. CHURCH · THE BUILDING · DUSK

Church benches hold dust.  
He steps to the **eastern wall** — and the **Keeper** leads him, through the book of the church, to the **Stay**.

**THE KEEPER’S LAST LOOK**: Look over there, at the arched roof.

### SCENE 8 · EXT. MILLER REACH · DUSK

The two ends of the desert. sand. maybe it’s the end of the world.

Every step heralds the wind, leaning in at every crest of the dune.

### SCENE 9 · INT. BROKEN BOOKS · THE KEEPER · DUSK

Pages turning. The word in the corner — **Stay**.  
He presses the page — as if reading his own obituary.

### SCENE 10 · INT. 4D TESSERACT · THE KEEPER · DUSK

The jump is the real answer to the question —  
- as against the anchor,  
- his hands turn paper,  
- the ink blackens another page,  
- the book further becomes a world.

**THE KEEPER** (the end of the jump, with the same gesture the player makes) — **LETTERBOX 2.39:1** on.

**HIM** (with the light of it behind him): I guess it’s not just the ancient wood that breathes fire.

**THE KEEPER**: I’ll keep it; it stays.

## Act Three — The Reading (1:12–1:42)

### SCENE 11 · INT. 1983 BRANCH · 11:06 · NIGHT

**VISUAL**: the pink CRT’s overlay blinds everything — only a Walkman under the arm can hear.

A signal — every RF pulse: **CEILING PULLS AWAY** — the crack in the ceiling.

### SCENE 12 · INT. BOOKKEEPER · THE ARCH · NIGHT

The bookkeeper dusts the frame.  
Time does not crack — 1983 is not a month.

### SCENE 13 · INT. UNDECIDED FRAMED · THE FURNACE · NIGHT

**THE FURNACE’S DRAW**: charged 4 seconds on a copper spear.  
The interruption — an eye which was in the furnace all the time.  
**He holds** — the spark falls. In minutes, not days.

He does **not** blow.

### SCENE 14 · INT. TIMELINE GOVERNOR · THE FREEZE · NIGHT

Everyone is greyed out — while his stance bar alone burns.  
**Hold 4** — the interrupt pierces into the core of the freeze.

### SCENE 15 · INT. CHAOS FRINGE · THE WAY · NIGHT

The neon concubine hedges. The door sees them —  
the door you remember, but cannot identify.

### SCENE 16 · INT. FINAL TRIAL · THE TOUR · NIGHT

To the very floor, sitting down.  
**WORLD ANCHOR EYES** — other doors, one animation. **Don’t Skip** the credits.

Forty-nine again — **the poster wall lights up all at once**.

## Coda — The Next Door (after the full credits, 40 minutes)

**CUT TO BLACK.**  
Silence. only one layer sub-bass, low enough to feel through the floor — same frequency as E2→A2.

**FADE IN**: INT. THE ANCHOR · ANOTHER TOWN · DAWN

A stranger in a strange coat (a hand that doesn’t belong to a human hand — a fox’s) — who has misplaced silver, gold, and his own eyes.

He hums a tune man has never heard.

**KEEPER’S GUEST POST**: Stay. Just one more.

The reader closes the PC, knowing the writer him- or herself is not the hero — **you will be the next reader.**

**TEN SECOND SHOT OF THE FLESH**: The jaw never settles into an expression. The eyes never close. The person never arrived. The book never ended.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

One more final frame lands on: the anchor, and a single word.

**WE ARE THE OTHER**  
**THE EMBER → NEXT**

**END.**
