## Motif design (Leitmotif)

- **Anchor theme**: four descending tones D–A–F–D, gold timbre, bass trombone + bell
- **Stay motif**: ascending minor third, piano, only on the wastes / the shelf
- **Upside motif**: whole-tone cluster, tape saturation
- **Echo motif**: one 1-second micro-sample per POI, layered into #23
